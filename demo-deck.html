Why we built this

Voice is now a bank’s weakest channel

  • Cloning is trivial. Seconds of a customer’s voice → a clone that authorises a transfer.
  • Most vishing needs no deepfake. A real human coerces a real customer — the larger share of losses.
  • The money moves during the call. Post-call detection is too late.
  • Millions of calls, many languages. Humans can’t screen them.

Today’s controls fail

× OTP — the victim reads it out under pressure.

× Voice biometrics — a good clone passes; a coerced caller passes.

× Human agents — can’t hear a deepfake or catch a practised scammer.

DHWANI-KAVACHVOICE OTP
Demo 5 of 6 · the hard problem

Play a clone through a speaker, and state-of-the-art detectors go blind.

clone file→ phone speaker→ air→ mic / IVR

The air hop smears the very micro-artifacts every artifact detector hunts for. This is the #1 real-world bypass — and most detectors read the replay as cleaner than the original.

A false all-clear on the exact attack fraudsters actually use.

DHWANI-KAVACHTHE REPLAY PROBLEM
Demo 5 of 6 · our answer

So we stopped fighting the artifact — and asked a simpler question

Not “is this synthesis perfect?” but “is this a live voice, or a playback?”

  • A small speaker physically cannot reproduce the low band a chest-resonant human voice carries at a real mic — nor the top octave (the speaker+air path rolls it off).
  • Two cheap spectral ratios catch exactly that. No model, no training — physics.
  • Either the neural model catches the synthesis, or this catches the replay. The one thing it will never do is silently trust a speaker playback.
